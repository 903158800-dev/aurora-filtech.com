Forming Fabric (Forming Belt)

Industrial forming fabrics for paper machines and non-woven fabric forming machines. Available in 1-layer, 1.5-layer, 2-layer and 3-layer constructions with anti-hydrolysis yarns.

Forming fabrics are precision-woven monofilament belts that form and dewater the fibre web on paper machines, belt conveyors and non-woven fabric forming machines. Aurora Filtech supplies 1-layer, 1.5-layer, 2-layer and 3-layer constructions with anti-hydrolysis yarns for long service life.

Applications

  • Paper mills — paper forming and drying-section support
  • Non-woven fabric forming machines — spunlace, needle-punch and airlaid lines
  • Belt conveyors and graphite flake forming lines

Specifications

  • Constructions: 1-layer, 1.5-layer, 2-layer, 3-layer
  • Yarn: polyester (PET) monofilament, anti-hydrolysis option
  • Seam: pin seam, clipper seam or endless on request
  • Edges: sealed or reinforced

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a forming fabric?

A forming fabric is a woven monofilament belt used on paper machines and non-woven forming machines to form and dewater the fibre web at the wet end of the process.

Which layer construction should I choose?

1-layer fabrics offer maximum drainage, 2-layer and 3-layer constructions provide a smoother surface and better stability for fine paper and nonwoven products. We recommend based on your machine and product.

Do you offer anti-hydrolysis forming fabrics?

Yes. Anti-hydrolysis monofilament yarns extend fabric life in humid, hot forming sections. Tell us your operating conditions and we will recommend the right yarn.
