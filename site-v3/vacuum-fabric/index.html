Vacuum Filter Fabric (Vacuum Filter Belt)

Vacuum filter fabrics for vacuum belt filters and vacuum disc filters in power plant FGD, phosphoric acid and mining filtration.

Vacuum filter fabrics are engineered for vacuum belt filters and vacuum disc filters in demanding dewatering duties — power plant FGD gypsum dewatering, phosphoric acid filtration and mining plant filtration.

Applications

  • Power plant FGD gypsum dewatering on vacuum belt filters
  • Phosphoric acid and chemical process filtration
  • Mining plant filtration — concentrates and tailings on vacuum disc filters

Frequently Asked Questions

Which material is best for FGD gypsum dewatering?

For power plant FGD duties we typically recommend anti-hydrolysis polyester or PVDF vacuum fabrics depending on temperature and pH. Send us your process data for a recommendation.

Do you supply fabrics for vacuum disc filters?

Yes. We cut and seam vacuum filter fabrics to fit vacuum disc filter sectors, with custom sizes for every model.

Can vacuum filter belts be made endless?

Yes. Vacuum belt filter fabrics can be supplied endless or with pin seams, with sealed or reinforced edges.
