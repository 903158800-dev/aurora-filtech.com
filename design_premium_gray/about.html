About Aurora Filtech

Pioneering Advanced Filter Media Technology Since 1990

Who We Are

Leading the Industry.

Aurora Filtech is a premier group company with three specialized plants and over a decade of dedicated experience in the filtration industry. We focus on researching and developing advanced filter media using the most cutting-edge materials to meet the rigorous requirements of our global customers.

Unlike many competitors, we own our proprietary monofilament technology. This capability allows us to produce high-performance yarns—including PPS, TEFLON, and anti-hydrolytic variants—in-house. This vertical integration ensures we can strictly control quality from the raw material stage.

Manufacturing Excellence

State of the Art Facilities

German Technology

Equipped with 100+ imported looms and computer-controlled systems from Germany and Sweden to ensure uniform tension and flawless fabric surfaces.

Heat Setting

Our 7-meter wide heat setting machine features 2 heating modes. With rollers up to 80cm in diameter, we achieve larger stretching strength.

Automated Jointing

Full-automatic joint machines save time and offer greater accuracy than manual labor, ensuring the joint is 100% perfect.
