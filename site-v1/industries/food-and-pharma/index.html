Food and pharma lines need belts that release cleanly and fabrics that meet the standards. Aurora supplies food-grade square mesh drying conveyors, anti-static dryer fabrics for food belt dryers, non-stick PTFE belts for vacuum belt dryers (liquid medicine, food pastes) and noodle drying lines, plus spiral press belts for juice and palm-oil pressing.

Food belt dryers — anti-static

Food-grade anti-static dryer fabrics for belt dryers — no dust cling, no product wrap-up, stable air permeability between washes.

Vacuum belt drying (liquid medicine, extracts)

Non-stick PTFE belts for multi-layer vacuum belt dryers — mesh belts for evaporation, solid belts for pastes — the standard duty for liquid medicine and food extracts.

PTFE and polyester drying belts for noodle and strip-product drying lines — non-stick surfaces, anti-static options.
