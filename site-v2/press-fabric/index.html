Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose a press filter fabric?

The choice depends on your filter type, particle size, temperature and chemistry. As a guide, mono-filament fabrics give easy cake release, multi-filament fabrics give finer retention. We recommend per application.

Do you supply cloths for membrane filter presses?

Yes. We weave and cut membrane filter fabrics for membrane plate presses, including centrate drainage and seal-edge designs.

Can the cloths be made seamless?

Barrel-neck and seamless cloths are available for full-plate designs on request.
