Wood Flour Sieving Mesh for WPC

Wood-plastic composite (WPC) lines classify wood flour on vibrating, rotating or oscillating screens — typically 40 to 140 mesh, with 60 and 80 mesh the common cuts. Aurora SM polyester square mesh gives stainless-steel precision at lighter weight and lower cost on these dry, low-abrasion duties, supplied as cut panels edged to your frame or as re-mesh for circular sifters.

By duty

Mesh count selection

40–140 mesh for WPC flour; aperture and open area per count in our spec table.

Panels & re-mesh

Cut and edged panels for gyratory / oscillating sifters; re-mesh service for SWECO-type circular separators.

Fines recovery on panel lines

Sifting mesh for fines streams beside the main (roller / disc) screen lines.

Platform note. Popular in North American WPC and panel plants; also supplied for general flour and powder sieving.
