Wood-based Panel Belts

From the forming line to the press outfeed, every deck of an MDF, OSB or particleboard line runs on woven and spiral fabrics. Aurora supplies the belts that matter at each stage — forming and pre-press, the drying decks, the biomass dryers — cut to the common plant platforms.

By process stage

Mat forming & pre-press

Anti-static fabrics

On MDF, OSB and particleboard forming lines, resin dust and high-speed friction charge the belt until fibers cling and mark the board. Anti-static monofilament drains the charge in the yarn itself; the spiral-linked enhanced seam survives the pre-press nip.

Dryer Fabric →

Pre-press belt, filmed on an MDF line

Our anti-static belt working the pre-press section of a Chinese MDF plant (Dieffenbacher-type line) — clean release and no dust cling, filmed as supplied.

Dryer Fabric →

Release-critical duties — 4106S silicone-coated

Panel lines only

Where resin-laden product must release cleanly, 4106S coats a single-layer fabric with high-temperature silicone — a version supplied specifically for MDF and board production.

Dryer Fabric →

Strand & particle belt dryers

Multi-layer belt dryers move OSB strands and particles on open spiral fabrics — no filler yarns, up to ~1000 CFM, so hot air passes through belt and product instead of fighting it.

Spiral Fabric →

Biomass & pellet drying

Fine square mesh belts for wood chip, shavings and pellet dryers — heat-set polyester that stays flat and stable to 180 °C; compatible with / alternative to OEM mesh belts.

Polyester Square Mesh →

Press & cooling decks

PTFE belts for release-sensitive press-outfeed duties and spiral belts for cooling and conveying decks.

Platform note. Belts dimensioned for the common plant platforms — Dieffenbacher, Siempelkamp / Büttner, Swiss Combi-type belt dryers and Chinese line builders.
