FGD Filtration & Baghouse Media

A power plant filters at both ends of the boiler: flue gas through the baghouse, and gypsum / sludge through the vacuum and press filters. Aurora supplies both — PPS dust bags for the baghouse, and the 617 / 623 vacuum fabric pair for limestone-gypsum FGD dewatering.

By process stage

FGD gypsum — 617, the economical workhorse

Low permeability

A multifilament weft takes air permeability into a fine band — right for limestone-gypsum FGD dewatering on horizontal vacuum belt filters, and the low-cost entry point.

Vacuum Filter Fabric →

FGD gypsum — 623, the upgrade

Mono surface · scraper-resistant

Surface 100% monofilament with the multifilament buried on the machine side: the gypsum cake releases cleanly, the surface resists the scraper, and campaigns run well above 617. Thicker than 617 — check filter clearances.

Vacuum Filter Fabric →

Fly-ash & coal-ash slurries

The same low-permeability constructions handle fly-ash (coal-ash) slurries that need a tight cloth.

Baghouse dust collection

PPS felt filter bags (membrane options) for coal-fired flue gas; PET bags for biomass and generic duties.

Sludge & wastewater (belt press)

PB woven press belts and SP-P spiral belts for dewatering FGD and plant sludges.

Platform note. Bags and fabrics sized to the common FGD and baghouse platforms.
