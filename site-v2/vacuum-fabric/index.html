Frequently Asked Questions

Which material is best for FGD gypsum dewatering?

For power plant FGD duties we typically recommend anti-hydrolysis polyester or PVDF vacuum fabrics depending on temperature and pH. Send us your process data for a recommendation.

Do you supply fabrics for vacuum disc filters?

Yes. We cut and seam vacuum filter fabrics to fit vacuum disc filter sectors, with custom sizes for every model.

Can vacuum filter belts be made endless?

Yes. Vacuum belt filter fabrics can be supplied endless or with pin seams, with sealed or reinforced edges.
