Frequently Asked Questions

Which filter bag material suits a coal-fired power plant?

PPS felt bags are the standard choice for coal-fired baghouses, offering sulphur and heat resistance. PTFE membrane PPS bags are used where finer emissions limits apply.

Do you make custom bag sizes?

Yes. Every dust filter bag is sewn to your tube sheet diameter and length, with snap-band, flange or ring tops.

Are anti-static filter bags available?

Yes. Anti-static felt bags are available for explosive dust applications.
